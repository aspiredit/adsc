When a child is diagnosed with autism, fathers often find themselves navigating unfamiliar territory. Dads can sometimes feel like they're standing on the sidelines. A few months ago, I had the privilege of connecting with Jamar Whitehurst, founder and host of the Puzzled Dads Podcast. From our very first conversation, it was clear that Jamar shares a passion for creating spaces where fathers feel seen, heard, and encouraged. Through his podcast, he brings together caregivers, professionals, and fathers from all walks of life to discuss autism, fatherhood, advocacy, faith, and the everyday realities of raising children with special needs. His thoughtful conversations provide practical wisdom and encouragement to fathers on the autism parenting journey.

At the Autism Dads Social Club, we believe that fathers are stronger when they have opportunities to learn from one another and build meaningful relationships. Jamar's work reflects that same vision. We recently sat down with him to learn more about the inspiration behind the Puzzled Dads Podcast, how his own experiences have shaped its mission, and what he hopes every father takes away from each episode. His story is a powerful reminder that while autism may introduce unexpected challenges, it also creates opportunities for growth, deeper connection, and a brotherhood built on shared experience.

## What Inspired Me to Launch the Puzzled Dads Podcast?

**Jamar Whitehurst:**

When I began looking for support and a deeper understanding of autism, I noticed something important: many of the available spaces seemed centered around mothers and women. Those communities are valuable and needed, but I did not see many places created specifically for fathers.

That need inspired me to create the Puzzled Dads Podcast: a space where fathers could feel comfortable having honest conversations, sharing their experiences, and realizing they are not alone on this journey.

As I grew into my role as an autism dad, I realized that many of us carry questions we do not always know how to ask out loud. There are moments of joy, fear, confusion, pride, and growth — sometimes all in the same day. I wanted to understand my son better, advocate for him better, and become the kind of father he needed me to be. That personal desire became part of a larger purpose: helping other dads feel seen, supported, and less alone. Out of that desire, the Puzzled Dads Podcast was born.

## How My Journey Shaped the Podcast & My Mission

**Jamar Whitehurst:**

My journey as an autism dad has shaped every part of this podcast. I do not come to the microphone as an expert; I come as a father who is still learning. I have experienced the highs and lows, the unexpected lessons, and the moments that challenged me to see parenting through a different lens.

Autism has taught me to slow down, pay closer attention, and recognize that progress does not always look the way we expect it to look. Through the podcast, I share my own story while encouraging other fathers to keep learning, growing, and giving themselves grace along the way.

Alongside those lessons, prayer has become a steady part of how I approach this work. I open each episode that way because it helps center the atmosphere and reminds me to let my faith guide the conversation.

There have been times when I felt unsure, overwhelmed, or afraid of what I did not understand. But there have also been moments when my son showed me strength, patience, and perspective that changed me. Those experiences pushed me to create a space where dads can talk honestly about what we feel, what we fear, and what we are still figuring out along the way.

## What Other Fathers Have Taught Me Through This Podcast

**Jamar Whitehurst:**

One of the greatest gifts of hosting the podcast has been the conversations with other fathers. Every dad has a different story, but there is a common thread of love, responsibility, and the desire to do right by our children. Some fathers speak from a place of experience. Others are still working through the early stages of diagnosis, therapy, school meetings, communication challenges, or questions about the future. In each conversation, I am reminded that none of us has to walk this road alone.

These conversations have taught me that vulnerability is not weakness. For many dads, talking about fear, frustration, confusion, or grief does not always come naturally. But when we make room for honesty, we also make room for healing. I have learned that fathers need spaces where they can admit they are still learning, celebrate small victories, and be encouraged by someone who understands the journey.

## What I Hope Listeners Take Away

**Jamar Whitehurst:**

My hope is that every episode leaves listeners with encouragement, understanding, and a sense of connection. I want dads to know that it is okay to have questions. It is okay to feel puzzled. It is okay to need support. Being an autism dad does not mean having everything figured out; it means being willing to keep showing up for your child, your family, and yourself.

I also hope listeners walk away with practical wisdom and a deeper appreciation for their children. Whether an episode focuses on advocacy, communication, school, sports, parties, faith, or everyday family life, the heart of the podcast remains the same: helping dads feel seen, supported, and equipped one piece at a time.

The Puzzled Dads Podcast is personal because this journey is personal. It is reflective because fatherhood has a way of teaching us when we are willing to listen. And it is meaningful because every story shared has the potential to encourage another dad who may be wondering if anyone else understands what he is going through. My answer is simple: yes, we do — and we are putting the puzzle together one piece at a time.

## One Conversation Can Change Everything

The journey of raising an autistic child is filled with questions, unexpected challenges, and remarkable moments of joy. Through the Puzzled Dads Podcast, Jamar Whitehurst is reminding fathers that they don't have to navigate those experiences alone. By creating a space rooted in honesty, faith, and authentic conversation, he is helping dads find the encouragement and community they need to become even stronger advocates for their children.

At the Autism Dads Social Club, we are grateful for leaders like Jamar who are investing in fathers and strengthening families one conversation at a time. His message echoes our own mission: when fathers are supported, families are strengthened, and communities become more connected.
