When a child is diagnosed with autism, fathers often find themselves navigating unfamiliar territory. Dads can sometimes feel like they're standing on the sidelines. A few months ago, I had the privilege of connecting with Jamar Whitehurst, founder and host of the Puzzled Dads Podcast. From our very first conversation, it was clear that Jamar shares a passion for creating spaces where fathers feel seen, heard, and encouraged. Through his podcast, he brings together caregivers, professionals, and fathers from all walks of life to discuss autism, fatherhood, advocacy, faith, and the everyday realities of raising children with special needs. His thoughtful conversations provide practical wisdom and encouragement to fathers on the autism parenting journey.

At the Autism Dads Social Club, we believe that fathers are stronger when they have opportunities to learn from one another and build meaningful relationships. Jamar's work reflects that same vision. We recently sat down with him to learn more about the inspiration behind the Puzzled Dads Podcast, how his own experiences have shaped its mission, and what he hopes every father takes away from each episode. His story is a powerful reminder that while autism may introduce unexpected challenges, it also creates opportunities for growth, deeper connection, and a brotherhood built on shared experience.
